Strategy Tips

  • Seasonal items: Pre-order pumpkin and holiday items aggressively. They sell out fast.
  • Perishables: Order conservatively. Unsold units incur 20% waste cost.
  • Stockouts: After week 8, repeated stockouts permanently damage demand.
  • Price elasticity: INELASTIC items can handle higher prices. ELASTIC items collapse if overpriced.
  • Crew morale: Low morale reduces throughput. Pay well and staff appropriately.
  • Trend signals: Watch for social media alerts 1-2 weeks before demand spikes.
  • Fan Favorites: Sell out seasonal items to earn +20% demand next year.

Game Phases

  • Week 1-8: Learning period. Stockouts don't permanently hurt demand.
  • Week 13: 30 SKU slots unlock. Summer seasonal items available.
  • Week 27: 40 SKU slots unlock. Fall/pumpkin season begins.
  • Week 40: Winter holiday items available (Jingle Jangle!).

KPI Targets

  • Gross Margin: Target 28%+ for healthy operations
  • Waste/COGS: Keep below 8% by managing perishables carefully
  • Stockout Rate: Keep below 10% to maintain demand
  • Labor/Revenue: Target under 15% for efficiency
  • Rent/Revenue: Target under 25% for profitability
